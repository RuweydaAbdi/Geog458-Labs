# Study area & dates
The study area I chose was puget sound and seattle area, I chose this region because I grew up in Seattle, and I have done much research associated with puget sound and Seattle, I chose this because I was most familiar with the area. The day I chose was May 25th to 28th 2024. I chose these days to analyze because Memorial day (a holiday) was observed on May 27th. I thought it would be interesting to see vessel traffic during a holiday weekend, what happens just beforem, during, and after memorial day. Time zone: AcessAIS timestamps are UTC, times in this READme are Pacific time
AcessAIS Source: Data from https://marinecadastre.gov/accessais/ From the U.S coast guard MarineCadastre partnership.
How I kept the dataset manageable: I kept it under 2gb, and double checked boundaries as i drew out my region, I also only requested a few days. This kept the CSV file small and easily to convert in the google colab to get my JSON files all small enough to complete this project.

# Interpretation
The traffic shown on my map is the majority of other vessels, (1,635) out of the total of 1,739 routes/paths. For the other categories, there are 58 passenger, 43 cargo, and 3 tanker paths. The dates I chose include the memorial weekend of 2024. An additional source: https://www.boat-ed.com/blog/memorial-day-boating-dangers-how-to-stay-safe/
Tells us that the waterways memorial day can be very chaotic, and crowded. This makes sense to me since after observing the data, I noticed so many classing paths ,Passenger paths bounce from places such as edmounds to kingston, seattle to winslow, clinton to mukilteo, port orchard to bremerton and etc.  and i also saw paths that stop halfway and rotate back. Unfortunately the dates I chose isn’t an accurate comparison of before and after the holiday since those few days I downloaded are too few and likely show us the preparation of the holiday and the return phases. Passenger routes/ traffic showed us that it was the busiest on May 28th from 10-11am pacific, This may mean people are traveling back home after the holiday(pacific). Another busiest day was on May 26th from 3 - 4pm Pacific, which tells us that people were traveling late afternoon to most likely return home after the holiday. I also noticed that the least busiest day was may 26th 2-3am pacific. Overall a lot of daytime traveling. I also want to note that cargo traffic was the lightest on May 25th.
